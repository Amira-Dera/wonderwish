How a great .LY name is made

A real example — using bit.ly to show the thinking behind the name.
Three steps. One idea. A domain that became a brand.

STEP 1 — THE WORD

bit

Three letters. One syllable. The word does half the work before the product even loads.

3 letters　 1 syllable
Universally understood

STEP 2 — ADD .LY

bit.ly

The extension doesn’t follow the name — it finishes it. Remove “.ly” and you don’t have a brand anymore. That’s the ideal outcome.

bit + .ly → One complete idea

STEP 3 — THE RESULT

bit.ly

The address is the brand. Every shortened link is free advertising — at billion-link scale.

  • Used by millions of marketers
  • Billions of clicks tracked
  • Active since 2008

The takeaway: bit.ly didn’t need a tagline to explain what it does. The domain does the work — reinforcing the brand every single time a link is shared, clicked, printed, or spoken aloud.
